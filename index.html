We begin by understanding your current level, goals, and learning needs. From there, we can choose the course or learning path that fits you best. If you're not sure where to start, I'll help you find the right place based on your goals and level.

Lessons are delivered online through platforms such as Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams. You can also book lessons with me through Preply. Depending on your needs, you can choose one-to-one lessons or small-group classes, with each lesson adapted to your level and goals.

My lessons focus on using English, not simply studying it. Grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, speaking, and writing are taught with practical communication in mind, so you can apply what you learn outside the classroom.

You’ll receive guidance and feedback throughout your learning journey. I’ll help you identify areas to improve, correct important mistakes, and adapt lessons to your needs so that you always know what to work on and how to improve.
About Me

Who is Ahmed?

Hi, I'm Ahmed, an English teacher passionate about helping learners become confident and independent English users. I believe English should be more than grammar rules and exercises. It should help you communicate, express your ideas, and use the language naturally in real-life situations. My lessons combine practical communication with grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, speaking, and writing, adapted to each learner’s level and goals. My goal is simple: to help you make English a natural part of your life, not just another subject you study.
